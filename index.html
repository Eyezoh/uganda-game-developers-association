About us

We’re building a tight-knit community that brings the Ugandan games industry together.

Uganda has the talent, the stories and the appetite — what it has lacked is a shared table. The UGDA exists so that studios, freelancers, publishers and schools stop working in isolation and start moving as one industry.

01

Build the community

Regular meetups, a members’ Discord and an open directory so developers across Kampala, Gulu, Mbarara and the diaspora can find each other.

02

Connect the ecosystem

We build bridges to government, telecoms, banks, investors and the wider creative sector so that games are understood as an industry, not a hobby.

03

Grow global reputation

Ugandan pavilions and delegations at international events, plus a press kit programme that puts local titles in front of publishers and press.

04

Run the events

Trade missions, network lunches, game jams and the annual Uganda Game Awards — the moments where the industry actually meets.

05

Mentor the next wave

The Uganda Game Mentors programme pairs emerging developers with senior practitioners for six months of structured, practical guidance.

06

Measure the industry

The Uganda Games Monitor is our annual research report on studio count, revenue, employment and skills gaps — the numbers advocacy needs.

Our members

Over 60 members across three divisions

Membership is open to any company or individual making games in Uganda — from a two-person mobile studio to a university programme. Members are grouped into divisions so that support and advocacy actually fit the work.

  • A vote on the association’s direction and priorities
  • Discounted stands at trade shows and the annual awards
  • Access to the mentor programme and the members’ directory
  • Legal, tax and publishing-contract guidance clinics
