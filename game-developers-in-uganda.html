Guide

Game developers in Uganda

Studios, teams, events and support for game development in Uganda, from public reporting, with sources.

Updated 12 November 2026 by the Uganda Game Developers Association.

Overview

Uganda has a small, growing game development scene. Studios such as Kola Studios have made mobile games since 2013[1], and in March 2026 the government-backed Game Jam 2026 brought over 40 participants together to build original Ugandan games in 24 hours[2]. The Uganda Game Developers Association (UGDA), founded in 2026, brings the studios, developers, artists and educators in this scene together.

Studios and teams

Kola Studios

A mobile app and game development studio founded in 2013 by Okalany Daniel, Onono Jasper and Terry Karungi. Its game Matatu turns the Ugandan card game Amatatu into a mobile game, and passed 1,000,000 downloads on Google Play[1].

Teams from Game Jam 2026

  • Code Chaos — Endangered, a puzzle game about protecting endangered species (1st place)[2][5]
  • Loop Studio — Nyanya Mbisi, a fast-paced game inspired by Uganda’s Rolex street food (2nd place)[2]
  • Night Owls — Last Hope, a survival game about environmental resilience (3rd place)[2]
  • Code Rancheo — Dynasty[2]
  • Mazuka Interactive — Border to Boda[2]

This list comes from public reporting and is not a list of UGDA members. Know a Ugandan studio that should be here? Tell us.

Events

Game Jam 2026

Game Jam 2026 was launched in March 2026 at the Deep Tech Centre of Excellence. It was spearheaded by the Science, Technology and Innovation (STI) Secretariat, in partnership with Tech Anatomy, EDIC and Arudem[3][4]. Over 40 participants took part in the 24-hour hackathon, on the themes of conservation, food culture, transportation and heritage[2]. Participants continue to receive mentorship through Tech Anatomy to turn their ideas into commercial products[5].

Support for the sector

Speaking for the government at the Game Jam 2026 launch, David Gonahasa said Uganda has the talent to compete internationally, and that work is under way to improve digital infrastructure and funding for innovators[3]. Game developer Ronald Kayima described the jam as a way to unite Uganda’s fragmented gaming community and shift it “from consumption to creation”[4].

Games in Ugandan media

NBS Game Plus is a gaming programme presented by Danze Edwin, a gaming and esports consultant[4]. NBS Sport reported that the programme would broadcast a full breakdown of Game Jam 2026[2].

How to find or hire game developers in Uganda

  • Contact the UGDA and tell us what you need.
  • Follow game jams such as Game Jam 2026, where new teams show their work.
  • Approach studios directly, such as Kola Studios.
