What we run

Four kinds of event

Network lunches

Monthly, informal, members-first. The backbone of the community.

Game jams

Open to everyone. The main on-ramp for people new to the industry.

Trade missions

Shared pavilions and delegations at international shows, at member rates.

The awards

An annual, juried celebration of Ugandan games and the people behind them.
