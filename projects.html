Our projects

The programmes we are setting up to do the association’s work — mentorship, research, showcases and community.

Mentor programme

Pairing emerging developers with senior practitioners, with structured check-ins, a defined goal for each pairing and a demo day at the end.

Advocacy

Speaking for the sector

Beyond the programmes, representation is a standing part of our work. We intend to make the case for games to the Ministry of ICT, the Uganda Communications Commission, the tax authorities and the wider creative-economy bodies.

Priorities we expect to raise include payment-rail access for studios selling internationally, clearer tax treatment of digital exports, and recognition of game development within national creative-industry funding.

Our commitments:

  • An annual position paper published to members
  • A seat sought on creative-economy working groups
  • Member consultation before any public position is taken
  • An open record of every meeting held on the sector’s behalf
