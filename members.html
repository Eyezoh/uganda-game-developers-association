Membership

What membership will offer

We are building these with our first members.

01

A voice

A vote at the general meeting, eligibility for the board, and representation whenever the association speaks to government or the press.

02

Access

The members’ community space and directory, regular network events, and first call on delegation slots at international events.

03

Support

Mentor matching, contract and tax clinics, and press-kit review.

Fees

Membership bands

Student & solo

Nominal

For students, solo developers and teams of two. Full community access, mentor programme included.

Small studio

Standard

For teams of 3–15. Full voting membership, discounted event stands, contract and tax clinics.

Company

Full

For studios over 15 people and institutions. Everything above, plus priority delegation slots and partner introductions.

Fees will be set with the first members and published here.
