Membership

What you get

Membership is designed to pay for itself in the first year through events, contacts and reduced trade-show costs.

01

A voice

A vote at the general meeting, eligibility for the board, and representation whenever we speak to government or the press.

02

Access

The members’ Discord and directory, monthly network lunches, and first refusal on delegation and pavilion slots.

03

Support

Mentor matching, contract and tax clinics, discounted engine and middleware licences, and press-kit review.

Fees

Membership bands

Student & solo

Nominal

For students, solo developers and teams of two. Full community access, mentor programme included, no vote until year two.

Small studio

Standard

For teams of 3–15. Full voting membership, discounted event stands, contract and tax clinics.

Company

Full

For studios over 15 people and institutions. Everything above, plus priority pavilion slots and partner introductions.

Exact figures are confirmed annually at the general meeting. Contact us for the current rate card.
